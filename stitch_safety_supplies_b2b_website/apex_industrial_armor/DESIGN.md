## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-trust digital presence for an enterprise industrial safety supplier specializing in PPE, work gloves, industrial-grade respirators, and technical cleaning consumables (kain majun). 

The visual style is **Corporate Modern with High-Precision Industrial Polish**. Rather than leaning into cliché hazard-yellow or basic industrial tropes, it pairs rich, commanding royal purple tones with sterile, ultra-clean white surfaces and cool slate neutrals. This communicates premium build quality, certified laboratory standards, and institutional reliability.

### Target Audience & Psychological Profile
- **Procurement Directors & Supply Chain Managers:** Require clarity, rapid specification verification, certification proof, bulk RFQ paths, and total reliability.
- **EHS (Environmental Health and Safety) Officers:** Demand exact technical compliance, protection ratings (e.g., EN388, CE, NIOSH), and clear safety categorization.
- **Factory & Plant Operations Heads:** Look for durability, bulk packaging logistics, and material performance metrics.

### Visual Principles
- **Commanding Precision:** Dense technical information organized through meticulous hierarchy, sharp tabular data, and prominent certification chips.
- **Surgical Cleanliness:** Generous negative space surrounding products to emphasize hygiene, quality inspection, and spotless supply-chain integrity.
- **Engineered Confidence:** Controlled micro-interactions, robust structural containers, crisp ambient shadows, and purposeful purple accents driving high-intent conversion points.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep violet tones with cool industrial slates and pristine white canvases. Royal purple signifies technological advancement, premium quality control, and executive rigor.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#5B21B6` / `#4C1D95` / `#6D28D9`):** Anchors main actions, header bars, authoritative badges, and key navigation highlights.
- **Accent & Interaction (`#7C3AED` / `#8B5CF6`):** Drives dynamic focus states, primary call-to-action buttons, high-priority status indicators, and interactive links.
- **Surface Foundations (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`):** High-clarity backdrops. Pure `#FFFFFF` serves as the primary card surface to highlight product images cleanly. `#F8FAFC` provides subtle contrast for section backdrops, while `#F1F5F9` defines nested specs and data panels.
- **Structural Dividers (`#E2E8F0`):** Fine architectural line-work that frames cards, separates specs, and binds data grids without clutter.
- **Typography & Neutrals (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`, `#475569`):** Deep slate hierarchy ensuring strict AAA contrast for readability across specifications, part numbers, and certification matrices.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Success & Certified (`#059669`):** In-stock status, pass ratings, safety certifications.
- **Warning & Hazard (`#D97706`):** Chemical hazard notes, handling warnings.
- **Critical / Out of Stock (`#DC2626`):** Regulatory non-compliance warnings, depleted inventory alerts.

## Typography

The typographic pairing merges the confident modern geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with the utilitarian clarity of **Inter** for technical copy, specification sheets, and data tables.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines & Hero Callouts):** Features tight letter-spacing and sharp structural terminals that convey engineering authority without feeling sterile.
- **Inter (Body, Specifications, & Data):** Chosen for its exceptional legibility at micro scales, distinct numeral design (crucial for SKU codes, GSM weight metrics, and test certificates), and neutrality.
- **Tabular Numerals & Monospace accents:** For product codes (e.g., `MJ-WHT-100`, `GLV-NIT-EN388`), activate `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain aligned specification tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display multi-attribute industrial catalogs, structured comparison matrices, and bulk quotation workflows.

### Breakpoints & Fluid Grid
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max content width 1320px, 48px canvas margin, 24px gutters. Standard 3-column and 4-column product matrices.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, 32px canvas margin, 20px gutters. Adapts 4-column desktop listings into dual 2-column card structures.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column grid, 20px canvas margin, 16px gutters. Single column for complex specs, side-scrolling cards for certification carousels.

### Layout Principles
- Use vertical section pacing (`space-xl` scaled with multi-factor clamp padding) to separate distinct industrial business lines (e.g., Heavy Gloves vs. Lint-free Majun Rags).
- Technical data clusters utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tight, cohesive key-value pairing (e.g., tensile strength, thickness, packaging units).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid model of **crisp ambient shadows** and **low-contrast architectural outlines**. This avoids muddy dark drop shadows and delivers a clean, clinical clean-room atmosphere suitable for certified safety equipment.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base / Canvas):** Surface color `#F8FAFC` or `#FFFFFF`. Completely flat, non-elevated.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Data Panels):** Background `#FFFFFF` bordered by a 1px continuous hairline outline of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Selected Items):** Triggered when hovering product cards or bulk order items. Border transitions to `#6D28D9` (with 30% alpha). Shadow lifts to: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Action Bars & Overlays / RFQ Drawers):** Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a roundedness level of `2`, utilizing standard 8px (`0.5rem`) radiuses for interactive controls and 24px (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`) for primary cards and content surfaces.

- **Primary Cards & Containers (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Delivers a tailored, humanized finish to heavy industrial hardware listings, keeping the UI contemporary and approachable.
- **Controls & Form Inputs (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md`):** Buttons, RFQ number inputs, and search bars balance industrial utility with clickability.
- **Badges & Chips (`9999px` / Full Pill):** Safety certifications (CE, ANSI, SNI), stock statuses, and technical pill tags feature full pills to establish immediate visual differentiation from rectangular data fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Bulk Quote / Contact Sales):** Solid `#7C3AED` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, subtle transition to `#6D28D9` on hover. Accompanied by micro ambient purple glow on focus.
- **Secondary (Download Spec Sheet / TDS):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a crisp 1px `#E2E8F0` border, text `#1E293B`, shifting to `#F1F5F9` on hover.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Inline text button in `#5B21B6` with an arrow glyph that shifts 3px on hover.

### Product & Safety Cards
- Standardized container built using `rounded-xl`, pure `#FFFFFF` background, and 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Cards feature three dedicated sections:
  1. **Image Staging Area:** Neutral `#F8FAFC` viewport with subtle inset containment for white cotton majun, textured nitrile gloves, or 3-ply industrial masks.
  2. **Technical Badge Shelf:** Pill tags displaying certifications (e.g., `EN 388: 4121X`, `100% Cotton Majun`, `N95 Equivalent`).
  3. **Action & Pricing Tier:** Clear bulk unit pricing (e.g., "IDR / Bal", "IDR / Box") with a high-visibility RFQ check action.

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Specs:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, font weight `label-md`.
- **Primary Brand Tier:** Tinted `#5B21B6` (10% fill) with `#5B21B6` text.
- **Compliance Verified:** `#ECFDF5` background with `#059669` text and a verification check icon.

### Form Inputs & RFQ Fields
- Inputs feature `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state switches border directly to `#7C3AED` with an outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`.

### Technical Specification Table
- Alternating row zebra fills utilizing `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
- Padded cells (`space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal) with 1px `#E2E8F0` divider rules, utilizing tabular figures for dimensional measurements, tensile strength, and packing weights.
